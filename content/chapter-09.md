# MODULE 9: CYBER SECURITY & DIGITAL SAFETY

## 9.1 Why Cyber Security Matters

Every day, thousands of Nigerians lose money, data, and privacy to cyber criminals. Understanding threats and protections is now as important as knowing how to use a computer.

**Cyber security** = protecting computers, networks, and data from unauthorised access, damage, or theft.

---

## 9.2 Common Cyber Threats

### 1. Virus
A self-replicating program that attaches itself to files and spreads when those files are opened. Can corrupt data or slow down the system.

### 2. Malware (Malicious Software)
A general term for any harmful software: viruses, worms, spyware, adware, ransomware.

### 3. Trojan Horse
Disguised as useful software (e.g. a "free game") but secretly performs harmful actions once installed.

### 4. Ransomware
Locks your files and demands payment to unlock them. The most devastating modern threat.

### 5. Phishing
Fake emails, SMS, or websites that trick you into giving passwords, bank details, or BVN. Usually urgent and alarming: *"Your account will be blocked in 24 hours — click here."*

### 6. Spyware
Secretly monitors your activity and sends information to attackers — keystrokes, passwords, browsing habits.

### 7. Worms
Self-replicating programs that spread across networks without user action.

### 8. Adware
Forces unwanted advertisements on your screen.

### 9. Keyloggers
Records every key you type — used to steal passwords and card details.

### 10. Social Engineering
Manipulating people psychologically to reveal confidential information. E.g. someone calling as "customer care" asking for your OTP.

---

## 9.3 Digital Safety Rules — The 12 Commandments

1. **Install reputable antivirus** and update it regularly.
2. **Update your operating system and apps** — updates patch security holes.
3. **Use strong, unique passwords** — minimum 12 characters, mix of upper/lower case, numbers, symbols. Never reuse the same password on multiple sites.
4. **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)** on all important accounts (email, bank, social media).
5. **Never share your OTP, PIN, or password** with anyone — not even "customer care."
6. **Verify before you click** — check the sender's real email, hover over links before clicking.
7. **Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking** — use mobile data or a VPN.
8. **Back up important files** — cloud and external drive.
9. **Log out** of accounts on shared computers.
10. **Lock your devices** with PIN, password, or fingerprint when stepping away.
11. **Beware of downloads** — only install software from official sources.
12. **Trust your instincts** — if an offer is too good to be true, it is a scam.

## 9.5 End-of-Module Quiz

**1. Which threat disguises itself as useful software?**
A) Worm
B) Trojan Horse
C) Adware
D) Spyware

**2. "Phishing" is best described as:**
A) A fishing game
B) Tricking users into revealing sensitive information
C) A type of antivirus
D) A network protocol

**3. Ransomware attacks typically:**
A) Speed up your computer
B) Lock your files and demand payment
C) Clean your hard drive
D) Improve your Wi-Fi

**4. The strongest password among these is:**
A) password123
B) Chima
C) Tr0ub4dor&3x!
D) 11111111

**5. OTP stands for:**
A) Only Trusted Password
B) One-Time Password
C) Online Transfer Protocol
D) Open Test Portal

### ANSWER KEY

**1. B** — Trojan Horses pretend to be useful software.

**2. B** — Phishing tricks users into giving away passwords/data.

**3. B** — Ransomware encrypts files and demands a ransom.

**4. C** — Length + mixed characters = strong password.

**5. B** — One-Time Password (used for 2FA).
